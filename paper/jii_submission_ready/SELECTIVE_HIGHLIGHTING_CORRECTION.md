# Selective red-highlighting correction

The previous reviewer-facing PDF was incorrect because the clean revised source wrapped essentially the entire manuscript body, all floats, all tables, algorithms, the abstract, and the highlights in a global red-color environment. That made unchanged material appear revised.

The corrected reviewer copy is generated by a word-level/source-structure comparison between the submitted source and the final clean revision. Its rules are:

- additions and materially changed text are red;
- unchanged text is black;
- figures preserve their normal semantic palette rather than being recolored red;
- unchanged table content remains black, while changed headings, labels, and values are red;
- deletions are omitted from the reviewer-facing copy;
- the optional full redline remains the deletion-inclusive audit artifact.

Some sections, including the abstract, theoretical analysis, assurance architecture, stress-test discussion, and new appendices, remain predominantly red because they were substantively rewritten or added. This is distinct from the former global-coloring error.

Verification performed:

- compiled with the Elsevier CAS source files and bibliography;
- 34-page searchable, unencrypted PDF;
- no unresolved citations or cross-references in the final LaTeX log;
- rendered and visually inspected across front matter, mixed unchanged/changed prose, tables, figures, theory, appendices, and bibliography;
- figures remain in their normal colors and the bibliography remains black.
